# Roadmap

Crowns & Centuries is a Crusader Kings-style grand strategy game in the browser, on a map of the
whole world. The player controls a country rather than a dynasty.

- **Time span:** 15 September 1066 to 1 January 2066. It is a sandbox: an end-date score screen,
  and you can keep playing after it.
- **Time:** real time with pause and five speeds.
- **Players:** single player against AI.
- **Characters:** a ruler, a council and commanders. No family trees.
- **Look:** the UI theme follows the player's era, and coats of arms become flags in later eras.

## Decisions

- **Stack:** TypeScript, Vite and React 19.
  - Custom WebGL2 map renderer, with a Canvas2D overlay for text.
  - The simulation is pure TypeScript with no DOM access. It is deterministic and seeded, so it
    also runs headless in Node for tests and balance runs.
- **Map:**
  - Miller projection, 16384×8192 map units.
  - About 3,000 land provinces, 498 sea zones and 35 lakes.
  - Borders are stored as shared arcs in a compact binary format (`src/shared/mapFormat.ts`).
- **Data:** generated by `tools/mapgen` from open data; see the README for sources. The output in
  `public/data` is committed.
- **Delivery:**
  - GitHub Pages, built by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` on every push.
  - A private claude.ai artifact, updated after each milestone.

### M1: First playable, with the economy and war

- Time: pause on space, speeds 1–5, a date display and alerts.
- Economy:
  - development, taxes, manpower
  - a budget screen and loans
  - buildings (farms, markets, barracks, castles, workshops, ports) with a construction queue
- Characters (basic):
  - ruler, heir and five council seats that give bonuses
  - commanders
  - ageing, death and simple succession
- Armies:
  - levies and men-at-arms (spearmen, archers, light cavalry, knights, horse archers, siege
    engines)
  - recruit, raise, merge and split
  - A* movement, supply and attrition
  - automatic embarking across seas
- Battles:
  - terrain, river crossings, commander skill, unit counters, morale and retreat
  - sieges with forts, garrisons and siege engines, then occupation (the occupation stripes are
    already in the fill shader)
- War and peace:
  - casus belli: claims, border conquest, or no reason at a stability cost
  - vassals join their liege's wars
  - war score and peace deals (cede provinces, pay gold, white peace), then truces
- AI:
  - builds and keeps a budget
  - picks targets, moves armies, besieges and retreats
  - accepts peace
- Save and load: gzipped into IndexedDB, plus file export.
- 1066 flavour: the Norwegian invasion of England and William's claim.

### M2: Diplomacy

- Opinion, alliances, non-aggression pacts, military access, guarantees and calls to arms.
- Vassals and tributaries, with loyalty, integration and independence wars.
- Fabricated claims, and coalitions against aggressive expansion.
- AI diplomacy.

### M3: Internal politics and the council

- Government types and laws: succession, crown authority, conscription and tax.
- Stability and legitimacy.
- Estates and interest groups, factions, revolts and civil wars.
- Elective and republican successions, and council tasks.
- Stylised procedural portraits.

### M4: Religion and culture

- Refined faith and culture data.
- Conversion and cultural integration.
- Holy wars (crusades and jihads).
- Religious heads, heresies and schisms.

### M5: Technology and eras

- About 100 techs across six eras, with research and diffusion between neighbours.
- Unit lines modernise: knights → pike and shot → line infantry → rifles → armour and air wings.
- The economy evolves: manors → trade → factories → modern industry.
- Governments evolve: absolutism, constitutional monarchy, republic, democracy, dictatorship,
  communism.
- Era themes go live and coats of arms become flags; nationalism and ideologies appear.

### M6: Navies, exploration and colonisation

- Fleets by era: galleys → cogs → carracks → galleons → ships of the line → ironclads →
  dreadnoughts → carriers and submarines.
- Naval battles, blockades and transports. These replace automatic embarking.
- Terra incognita for each country, explorers and map sharing.
- Colonising empty and native lands, and colonial subjects.

### M7: Events, decisions and espionage

- An event engine: triggers, a mean time before each event fires, and choices with effects.
- Events across all eras: plague, steppe hordes, the Reformation, printing, revolutions,
  industrialisation, and world wars that emerge from alliance blocs.
- Decisions to form nations: Spain, Great Britain, Italy, Germany, Russia, and restoring Rome.
- Spy networks: fabricate claims, sabotage, incite revolts, steal tech, assassinate.

### M8: Endgame and polish

- End-date score and a world ledger.
- Balance passes using headless AI runs of 1,000 years.
- Performance work, a tutorial and help, and optional audio.

## After every milestone

- Typecheck, lint, unit tests, build, and `npm run mapgen:validate` if the data changed.
- Take Playwright screenshots at world, Europe and county zoom, and review them.
- Commit and push; GitHub Pages redeploys.
- Rebuild the artifact (`npm run build && npm run artifact`) and republish it to the same claude.ai
  URL.

## Known gaps and ideas

- **Province names:** these are mostly modern.
  - Historical names exist for about 290 major cities.
  - Parts of Central Asia and the Americas still read like modern admin regions ("North-West
    Aktobe").
  - A later pass will add cultural names.
- **Close zoom:** the terrain is soft, at one texel per map unit. A detail texture or procedural
  grain would help.
- **Small realms:** these were dropped where a province is bigger than the whole realm (for
  example the Duchy of Naples).
